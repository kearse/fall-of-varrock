The **Forge** is an experimental anvil in the Mire working yard where smiths turn **rune gear into dragon gear** - the server's marquee coin-and-materials sink, and the reason runite bars matter at endgame.

## How to use it

Bring a **hammer**, the rune item, the runite bars and the fee, then use the rune piece on the Forge:

| Upgrade | Smithing | Runite bars | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rune scimitar → Dragon scimitar | 60 | 30 | 500,000 gp |
| Rune med helm → Dragon med helm | 60 | 30 | 400,000 gp |
| Rune full helm → Dragon full helm | 65 | 50 | 800,000 gp |
| Rune kiteshield → Dragon kiteshield | 70 | 70 | 1,500,000 gp |
| Rune platelegs → Dragon platelegs | 70 | 80 | 1,500,000 gp |
| Rune platebody → Dragon platebody | 75 | 100 | 2,500,000 gp |

Every bar consumed grants **50 Smithing XP**, so a platebody upgrade is 5,000 XP on top of the gear.

## Remember the armour gate

Forging dragon armour doesn't mean you can wear it - dragon requires the **Lord** title (2,000,000 gp rank). See [Titles & citizenship](/wiki/titles-and-citizenship). Plenty of smiths forge for profit and sell to Lords instead.

## Where the bars come from

Runite ore is in the castle cellar mine and Mire collection grounds (Mining 85); each runite bar needs 8 coal (Smithing 85, or Superheat Item). The Trading Post also stocks adamantite bars and coal if you'd rather buy your way in - see [Trading Post & the market](/wiki/trading-post-and-shops).
