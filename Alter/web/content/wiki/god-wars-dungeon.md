The **God Wars Dungeon generals** have returned - all four at once, rebuilt as a single
package. These are **shared-world camps**, not instances: each throne room holds its
general and three bodyguards, everything in the room is aggressive, and other players
can be fighting alongside (or against) you. Bring a team or bring courage.

## Getting there

Teleport portal → **Bosses** tab: **General Graardor**, **Commander Zilyana**,
**K'ril Tsutsaroth** and **Kree'arra** each land you at the edge of their room.
These rooms are **hostile ground** - the whole camp wakes up when you walk in.

## The four fights

| General | Bodyguards | The fight |
|---|---|---|
| **General Graardor** (Bandos) | Steelwill (mage), Grimspike (ranger), Strongstack (melee) | Crushing melee up close; his ranged **boulder barrage hits everyone in the room**. Pray Melee in his face, watch the barrage. His magic defence is enormous - bring melee or ranged. |
| **Commander Zilyana** (Saradomin) | Growler (mage), Bree (ranger), Starlight (melee) | The fastest attacker in the game - relentless melee, and 1-in-7 attacks she roots the room with an **instant lightning burst** (two magic hits on everyone, no projectile to dodge). Protect from Magic when she glows. |
| **K'ril Tsutsaroth** (Zamorak) | Balfrug (mage), Zakl'n (ranger), Karlak (melee) | Heavy slashes and a room-wide magic barrage - and **praying melee is a gamble**: he can smash through your prayer for up to 49, draining half your prayer points. Carries poison on every attack - bring antidotes. |
| **Kree'arra** (Armadyl) | Skree (mage), Geerin (ranger), Kilisa (melee) | **Flying - melee cannot touch him.** His gale blast hits the whole room for up to 71 ranged and **buffets everyone back a tile**; his magic volley is the softer roll. Ranged him down, Protect from Missiles. |

Kill the bodyguards first or fight in a corner - every caster and ranger in the room
will be on you from the moment you land.

Each throne room has its god's **altar**: **Pray-at** restores your prayer points in full, once
every **ten minutes** (the cooldown is shared by all four altars and survives logging out). The
altars take no bone offerings. The rooms are **multi-combat**, so bursts and barrages splash the
bodyguards.

## Loot - the godsword grind

Every general drops from his own donor table, with the chase items all Collection Log
entries:

- **Godsword shards 1/2/3** - 1/100 each from **every** general; combine three shards
  and a hilt at an anvil for a godsword.
- **Hilts** - Bandos / Saradomin / Zamorak / Armadyl, 1/125 from their owner.
- **Bandos armour** (chestplate/tassets 1/128, boots 1/64) from Graardor.
- **Armadyl armour** (helmet 1/64, chestplate/chainskirt 1/128) from Kree'arra.
- **Saradomin sword** (1/64), **Armadyl crossbow** (1/200), **Saradomin's light** from Zilyana.
- **Zamorakian spear** (1/100), **Staff of the dead** (1/250), **Steam battlestaff** from K'ril.
- **God pets** - each general drops his own at 1/1000.

Every kill rolls the general's drop table and counts toward `::kc`; bodyguards drop from the standard tables.
