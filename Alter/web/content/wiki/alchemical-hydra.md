The **Alchemical Hydra** is the third fight of the rebuilt roster — a four-phase chemical
monstrosity fought **solo in your own instance**. Its trick: the hydra takes **only a
quarter damage** until you lure it over the right chemical vent, and standing over the
*wrong* vent makes it hit harder. Positioning wins this fight.

## Getting there

Teleport portal → **Bosses → Alchemical Hydra** lands you by the lab rocks — **climb them**
(or type `::hydra`). Dying inside is a **safe death**.

## The four phases

The hydra changes form as its health drops, and each form has a **weakness vent**:

| Phase | HP | Special | Lure it over |
|---|---|---|---|
| Green (poison) | 100-75% | Poison pools | The **red** vent |
| Blue (lightning) | 75-50% | Walking lightning | The **green** vent |
| Red (fire) | 50-25% | Fire walls + tracking fire | The **blue** vent |
| Grey (enraged) | 25-0% | Poison pools | Nothing — it can't resist, but it hits ~50% harder |

Until the hydra stands over its weakness vent (they spew on a cycle — you'll see the
feedback messages), your hits are **quartered**. Standing over any *other* vent
**empowers it** (up to +50% damage), and standing on a spewing vent yourself burns you
for up to 20. Lure carefully.

## Attacks

- It alternates **ranged and magic every three attacks** (every single attack when
  enraged) — count and switch your overhead prayer.
- Every ninth attack is the phase's **special**:
  - **Poison pools** splatter around your tile — step off the splash zone.
  - **Walking lightning** — four bolts crawl toward you; keep moving, getting caught
    deals 20 and roots you.
  - **The fire phase** — it returns to the centre, pins you for a moment, walls off the
    room's edges with fire and sends a **tracking flame** chasing your footsteps. Keep
    walking; standing still gets you clipped, and standing in fire burns fast.

## Loot

Every kill drops **hydra bones** plus a solid coin/supply roll (dragon knives and
thrownaxes among them). The chase drops, all Collection Log entries:

| Drop | Rarity |
|---|---|
| Hydra's eye / fang / heart (brimstone ring pieces) | 1/60 each |
| Hydra tail / Hydra leather | 1/125 |
| Hydra's claw | 1/300 |
| Jar of chemicals | 1/500 |
| Ikkle hydra (pet) | 1/1000 |

Every kill rolls the drop table and counts toward `::kc`. After a kill, a fresh hydra rises in the chamber —
chain away.
