Varrock fell. Lumbridge holds. The **reconquest** is how the realm takes it back - and unlike campaigns and conquests, you don't need rank or coin to fight in it. This is the beginner's door into the war.

## Marches - the free warband

Roughly every half hour the **Knight-Captain musters a march**: a column of Knights of Lumbridge that sets out from the castle and pushes into one **district** of [Fallen Varrock](/wiki/fallen-varrock). The muster call is announced ~5 minutes ahead, and **any player can fight beside the column** - walk with it from the gate, or type `::march` to rally straight to it (you'll be asked to confirm if the column is already fighting on PvP ground).

- Marches are free to join but **cost the realm 150 war supplies** to launch. If the stores are dry, the march is called off and the whole server hears about it - go feed the [Quartermaster](/wiki/supplying-the-war).
- A won march pays every fighter a **share of the pooled spoils by contribution**, plus **1–3 Commendations** (see below).
- Marches **can fail**. Ten knights alone are often driven back - the realm learns to march with them.

The command ladder above the march is unchanged: **March (anyone) → Raid (Lord) → Campaign (Minister) → Conquest (King)** - see [The War explained](/wiki/the-war-explained).

## The district map - `::districts`

Fallen Varrock is split into four districts: **the Slums**, **the Old Market**, **the East Quarter** and **the Museum Quarter**. Each has a persistent **pressure meter** that won marches fill. Fill it and the district **BREAKS** - the occupiers' grip fails, announced realm-wide.

Broken districts matter beyond bragging rights: **each one weakens Varrock's garrison for the commanders**, shaving 10% off the kill quota of any campaign or conquest on the city. When all four are broken, **the Palace road lies open** - the stage for the King's conquest.

Check the map any time with `::districts`.

## Grand Marches & the Wardens

Every **8th march is a GRAND MARCH**: a bigger column, a louder muster call, and a real target - the district's **Warden**, a boss-tier defender waiting at the district's heart. Bring friends.

When a Warden falls, his **Warden's embers** pay out to the players who fought him: **guaranteed for the top damage dealer**, rolled at 1 in 3 for everyone else. Embers are **tradeable**, and they are the only source of the [war-forge's](/wiki/war-forging) helm tier. If the Grand March is driven back, the Warden lives - and the realm is told his embers stay cold in the enemy's grip.

## Commendations - your service record

**Commendations** are the war's service token, paid on every **won** march, campaign or conquest, scaled by your contribution (marches pay up to 3, campaigns up to 6, conquests up to 10). Two rules:

1. **They cannot be traded.** No coin buys another soldier's service record - this is the one ingredient of max gear you must earn yourself.
2. They're spent at the [Royal Smith](/wiki/war-forging), where the realm's best gear is made.

## The wanted captains - `::bounties`

Each district is run by a named captain on a long respawn - **Karn the Red** (the Slums), **Silas the Hollow** (the Old Market), **Vex of the Row** (the East Quarter) and **Grimjaw** (the Museum Quarter). Killing one pays a **100,000 coin bounty straight to your bank**, 2 Commendations and 25 War Effort - and rolls the captain's **signature weapon at 1 in 15**:

| Captain | District | Signature drop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Karn the Red | the Slums | Dragon claws |
| Silas the Hollow | the Old Market | Nightmare staff |
| Vex of the Row | the East Quarter | Armadyl crossbow |
| Grimjaw | the Museum Quarter | Dragon warhammer |

Their streets are deep-wilderness PvP ground - the risk is the content. Captain kills also count toward the Recruiting Sergeant's [rogue-hunting milestones](/wiki/fallen-varrock): every 10 / 50 / 250 / 1,000 rogue-family kills pays an escalating bounty (`::rogues` to check your tally).
