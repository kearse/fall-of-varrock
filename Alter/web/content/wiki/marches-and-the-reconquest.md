Varrock fell. Lumbridge holds. The war is fought *out* of the Last Free City against the camps, roads and ruins the enemy holds - and unlike campaigns and conquests, you don't need rank or coin to fight in it. This is the beginner's door into the war.

## Marches - the free warband

Roughly every half hour the **Knight-Captain musters a march**: a column of Knights of Lumbridge that sets out from the castle and strikes one target from the realm's list of hostile ground. The muster call is announced ~5 minutes ahead, and **any player can fight beside the column** - walk with it from the gate, or type `::march` to rally straight to it (you'll be asked to confirm if the column is fighting on wilderness ground).

- Marches are free to join and **launch automatically every cycle, no matter what** - they keep the war moving even on a quiet server, so the early game never stalls. A march **costs the realm nothing**: it never touches the Realm Supplies stockpile and is never called off for empty stores. (Realm Supplies are spent only by the commanders' campaigns and conquests; see [Supplying the war](/wiki/supplying-the-war).)
- A won march pays every fighter a **share of the pooled spoils by contribution**, plus **1-3 Commendations** (see below) and War Effort.
- Marches **can fail**. Ten knights alone are often driven back - the realm learns to march with them.

The command ladder above the march: **March (anyone) → Operation (Lord) → Campaign (Minister) → Conquest (King)** - see [The War explained](/wiki/the-war-explained). Rank only ever gates *starting* a war; joining one is open to every citizen.

## The targets - `::marches`

The Knight-Captain picks each march's target from the realm's board of hostile ground. Ordinary marches strike camps, roads and frontier threats; serious penetration into Fallen Varrock is reserved for campaigns, conquests and the story.

| Target | What holds it | Ground | Grand March? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Lumbridge goblin camp** | the goblin warband east of the castle | safe | no |
| **The Bandit Hideout** | the rogues' first camp, by the jail on the Draynor road | safe | **yes** - the Warden of the Hideout |
| **The Draynor road** | highwaymen bleeding the supply road on Draynor's southern edge | safe | no |
| **The Varrock outskirts** | marauders and Black Knights on the southern road, the risen dead nearest the gate | **wilderness** | **yes** - the Warden of the Southern Road |

`::marches` shows the board, which target is mustering or under attack, and when the next march sets out. The list grows as the war spreads (Port Sarim's docks and the deep-wilderness bandit camp are next).

## Grand Marches & the Wardens

Every **8th march is a GRAND MARCH**: a bigger column, a louder muster call, and a real target - one of the grand-eligible positions above, held by its **Warden**, a boss-tier defender waiting at the rally point. Bring friends.

When a Warden falls, his **Warden's embers** pay out to the players who fought him: **guaranteed for the top damage dealer**, rolled at 1 in 3 for everyone else. Embers are **tradeable**, and they are the only source of the [war-forge's](/wiki/war-forging) helm tier. If the Grand March is driven back, the Warden lives - and the realm is told his embers stay cold in the enemy's grip.

## Lord operations - `::operation <target>`

A **Lord** or higher may sponsor a march of their own on any target: `::operation bandit_hideout`. It is coin-funded (500,000 - the Lord's contribution to the war; not refunded), never spends Realm Supplies, and is just as public as the scheduled march - any soldier rallies to it with `::march`. Win it and the sponsor takes the commander's tithe from the spoils (`::claim`) on top of their fighter's share.

## Commendations - your service record

**Commendations** are the war's service token, paid on every **won** march, operation, campaign or conquest, scaled by your contribution (marches pay up to 3, campaigns up to 6, conquests up to 10). Two rules:

1. **They cannot be traded.** No coin buys another soldier's service record - this is the one ingredient of max gear you must earn yourself.
2. They're spent at the [Royal Smith](/wiki/war-forging), where the realm's best gear is made.

## The wanted captains - `::bounties`

Each district of [Fallen Varrock](/wiki/fallen-varrock) is run by a named rogue captain on a long respawn - **Karn the Red** (the Slums), **Silas the Hollow** (the Old Market), **Vex of the Row** (the East Quarter) and **Grimjaw** (the Museum Quarter). Killing one pays a **100,000 coin bounty straight to your bank**, 2 Commendations and 25 War Effort - and rolls the captain's **signature weapon at 1 in 15**:

| Captain | District | Signature drop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Karn the Red | the Slums | Dragon claws |
| Silas the Hollow | the Old Market | Nightmare staff |
| Vex of the Row | the East Quarter | Armadyl crossbow |
| Grimjaw | the Museum Quarter | Dragon warhammer |

Their streets are wilderness PvP ground - the risk is the content. Captain kills also count toward the Recruiting Sergeant's [rogue-hunting milestones](/wiki/fallen-varrock): every 10 / 50 / 250 / 1,000 rogue-family kills pays an escalating bounty (`::rogues` to check your tally).
