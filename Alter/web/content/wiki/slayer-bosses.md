The **Slayer bosses** are the high-Slayer lairs every OSRS player grinds for their best-in-slot
pieces, kept mechanically faithful. Three of them are gated by your **Slayer level**: the game
refuses the attack until you have it.

## Kraken (87 Slayer)

Portal → **Kraken** lands you at the boss pool in Kraken Cove. Four **tentacle whirlpools** ring
the big one — **Disturb** each tentacle to raise it (they spit for a little damage), then Disturb
the Kraken's whirlpool. The Kraken casts a magic bolt (max 28) — **Protect from Magic** and a
trident or the best spell you have. It never leaves its pool. When it dies the tentacles sink
and the pools reset a few seconds later.

**Uniques:** Trident of the seas (1/512), Kraken tentacle (1/400), Jar of dirt (1/1000), Pet
kraken (1/1000). 20 Boss Tickets.

## Cerberus (91 Slayer)

Portal → **Cerberus** lands by the west lair; there are three lairs in Taverley Dungeon's
depths. Cerberus melees up close (max 23) and fires **magic and ranged** (max 23) at range,
with three signature moves:

- **Triple attack** — magic, then ranged, then melee two ticks apart. Switch prayers in order.
- **Summoned souls** (below 400 HP) — three ghosts walk in and each fires its own style. Pray
  the matching overhead: blocked attacks cost **30 prayer** (15 with a spectral spirit shield),
  unblocked ones deal **30** through everything.
- **Lava** (below 200 HP) — three pools, one on your tile. Standing in one hurts every two ticks;
  the final flare hurts more. Step off.

**Uniques:** Primordial, Pegasian and Eternal crystals (1/512 each), Smouldering stone (1/512),
Jar of souls (1/1000), Hellpuppy (1/1000). 30 Boss Tickets.

## Thermonuclear Smoke Devil (93 Slayer)

Portal → **Thermonuclear Smoke Devil**, deep in the smoke devil dungeon (multi-way). Wear a
**facemask or Slayer helmet** — without one every attack is 18 unblockable smoke damage. With
one it spits a fast ranged attack (2 ticks, max 8); Protect from Missiles.

**Uniques:** Occult necklace (1/350), Smoke battlestaff (1/512), Dragon chainbody (1/1000), Pet
smoke devil (1/1000). 20 Boss Tickets.

## Skotizo (dark totem)

Portal → **Skotizo** lands at the Catacombs of Kourend altar. Bring a **dark totem** (Skotizo
drops totems himself, and pieces come from the catacombs) and use the altar's Teleport option —
or `::skotizo` — to be dragged into a **private instance** of his chamber. He melees (max 38)
and casts (max 38). Four **altars** around the room wake on their own; every awakened altar
**hardens him** — kill them (they're attackable) to put them back to sleep. When he dies you are
returned to the catacombs.

**Uniques:** Dark claw (1/25), Skotos pet (1/65), Uncut onyx (1/128), Jar of darkness (1/500);
always a hard clue and 1–3 ancient shards. 30 Boss Tickets.

## Demonic Gorillas

Portal → **Demonic Gorillas** drops you in the Crash Site Cavern (multi-way) with a dozen
gorillas. Each fights with one style at a time — **melee** when it can reach you, otherwise
**ranged** (green) or **magic** (purple), max 31 — and **switches style after three misses** or
three prayed hits. Every 50 damage it takes it **switches its own prayer** to block the style you
just used, so bring two styles. Its **boulder** lands on your tile every so often for a third
of your health — move when it raises its arms.

**Uniques:** Zenyte shard (1/300), Ballista limbs, Ballista spring, Heavy frame and Monkey tail
(1/500 each). 5 Boss Tickets per gorilla.

All uniques and pets are **Collection Log** entries; every boss counts toward `::kc`.
