Server-wide headlines - boss spawns, war captures, marches, events, Castle Wars horns - don't scroll away in your chat. They show in the **announcement ticker**: coloured lines stacked directly **above the chat box**, each with a diamond marker, newest at the bottom.

## How it works

- Headlines hold on screen for about a minute, then fade; up to three show at once.
- They **don't appear in the chat box at all** - the ticker is their home, so your chat stays clean for conversation.
- Each line keeps the colour of its event type (war red, victory green, gold for big wins).

## Tuning it

Open the client's plugin settings and find **Fall of Varrock Announcements**:

- **Show ticker** - turn the ticker off entirely.
- **Hide from chat box** - untick if you'd rather broadcasts also land in your chat history.
- **Lines shown / Hold time / Font size** - how many headlines stack, how long they stay, and how big they draw.

On a vanilla client the same broadcasts simply appear as chat lines instead - you miss nothing, just the presentation.
