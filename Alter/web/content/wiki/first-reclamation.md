**First Reclamation** is the first time the kingdom takes ground *back*. Every march before this one hit the enemy and went home. This time General Zo wants the southern approach to Fallen Varrock cleared - and then **held**: a permanent forward post at the old stone circle, in the shadow of the city walls.

It begins the moment [The North](/wiki/the-north) is complete: General Zo has decided surviving is no longer enough.

## The quest

1. **The brief** - General Zo in the castle hub. Not Varrock - the stone circle on the southern road. Marches reach it now and again and always leave; this time the realm will use the ground it wins. He will not send soldiers to ground their commander hasn't seen, so the reconnaissance is yours.
2. **Walk north** - no teleport for this one. Take the road toward Varrock: the roads are part of the lesson, and Rogue Knights, wandering enemies and the realm's own marches may all find you on the way. Three places to see, in order - the quest marks each one:
   - **The southern road** - where the road reaches the lower end of the outskirts battlefield. Somewhere for soldiers to regroup before approaching Varrock.
   - **The stone circle** - defensible ground overlooking the road. Step inside the ring.
   - **The gate approach** - the road just beyond the circle, in front of Varrock's south gate. The enemy presence is obvious even from here; taking the city itself will take far more than Lumbridge has.
3. **Report** - back to General Zo. "The position is usable. The city isn't." When you are ready, give the word.
4. **The Reclamation Column** - Zo launches a **public Grand March** on the Varrock outskirts (`::march` rallies you to it). The whole realm hears the call and anyone may fight beside the column - it is the normal [march system](/wiki/marches-and-the-reconquest): sixteen Knights of Lumbridge against the marauders on the far approach, the Black Knights on the road and the risen dead nearest the gate. **The column must win**, and you must have done a real share of the fighting (at least 1%). You do not need the killing blow or a particular kill - just fight.
   - *Driven back?* The quest stays live: speak with General Zo and he sends the column again as soon as the ground is clear. No wait, no reset - only the lost battle.
   - *Cleared without you?* Same: Zo needs you on that road, not behind it.
5. **Establish the Southern Watch** - when the line breaks ("The enemy line breaks. The southern approach to Varrock is clear."), return to the stone circle and **Capture** the standard at its heart. You raise Lumbridge's standard over the southern approach - the first forward position in twelve years.
6. **Debrief** - General Zo. The roads around the Watch are still hostile and the dead will certainly return - "but now when they do, we have somewhere to meet them." Then the question he has been waiting to ask: how many soldiers are inside that city, and how many do we have? *Quest complete.*

## The Southern Watch

The forward post is **shared** - it stands at the stone circle for everyone: the raised standard, three Knights of Lumbridge who hold the ring, and a **Field Quartermaster** who takes [war supplies](/wiki/supplying-the-war) exactly like his colleagues in Lumbridge and the Mire. Completing the quest earns the personal part:

- **Fast travel** - the **Southern Watch** row in the [portal's](/wiki/teleport-portal) War tab, General Zo's *"Send me to the Southern Watch."*, or `::southernwatch`. All three land inside the ring.
- **Varrock staging** - the Watch sits on the `varrock_outskirts` march target, on the doorstep of Fallen Varrock's own content and the northern Rogue Knight camps.

The ground around it stays contested on purpose: the outskirts remain a repeatable march target, the enemy lines still stage straight through the circle when a march is on, and the garrison fights whatever comes. Lumbridge holds a *foothold* here, not a safe zone - the danger tag says Hostile for a reason.

## Rewards

- **50 War Effort**
- **The Southern Watch** - fast travel, the Field Quartermaster post, the garrison
- The normal spoils of the won Grand March, paid by the war itself
- The next main quest, **A Kingdom Alone** - because the debrief ends where it must: *"We can't take Varrock." "Not alone."*
