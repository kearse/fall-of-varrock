Two fights the main story leaves behind for you to repeat. **Arrav**, freed, waits in the
fallen palace's west hall: talk to him (or `::zemouregal` / `::convergence`) and choose. Both are
private instances with safe deaths, a fifteen-minute clock and companions left at the door.

## Zemouregal

The Mahjarrat who broke Varrock, met again in his stolen palace — and this time Arrav fights
**beside you**, striking every few seconds for solid damage.

- **Melee 30** when you stand close; otherwise an **ice bolt** (28, holds you two ticks) or a
  **blood bolt** (26, heals him half of what lands).
- Every sixth attack: **"Rise, and serve!"** — two risen dead join him. Kill them or kite them.
- Every tenth attack: **Mahjarrat flare** — three ticks of warning, then 32 through prayer to
  anyone within three tiles. Step out.
- Below a third of his health he wrenches at Arrav's heart, stunning your ally for a spell.
- If Arrav falls, Zemouregal drinks 200 from the loss. Keep him alive if you can.

**Drops (every kill):** 3 Relics of old Varrock, 15 Varrock salvage, plus runes, bars, bones and
potions. **Chase:** *Arrav's axe* (1/150, cosmetic), *Mahjarrat notes* (two volumes, 1/40 each).
**Pays** 50 War Effort, 6 Commendations; a **Warden's ember** one kill in five.

## The Convergence

What the Fracture left behind: the buried conduits' manifestation, deepest in current
Senntisten. Magic only — bring the prayer and the restores.

- **Necrotic wave** 30, biting 5 prayer each hit.
- Every fifth attack: **grasping conduits** — a three-tick hold and 18 damage.
- Every ninth: **fracture pulse** — three ticks of warning, then 38 through prayer within three
  tiles.
- At two-thirds and one-third health it births **three echoes**.

**Drops (every kill):** 4 relics, 20 salvage and a **Warden's ember**, plus high runes, bars,
bones and potions. **Pays** 80 War Effort, 10 Commendations. Your Convergence
kill count is recorded — the *Fracture-Sealer* standing will hang on it.

`::kc` tracks both.
