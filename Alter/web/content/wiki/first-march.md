This morning the war came to Lumbridge. **First March** is where Lumbridge goes to the war. It begins on its own the moment [The Last Free City](/wiki/recruit-trials) is finished - Sergeant Damien has already told you who musters the columns.

## The quest

1. **Report to General Zo** - in the castle courtyard, beside Duke Horacio. He explains the **[March](/wiki/marches-and-the-reconquest)**: every half hour, ten Knights of Lumbridge walk out of the courtyard and hit something that deserves it - a camp, a road, a rogue position - and **anyone may march with them, no rank required**. The goblins that probed the gate this morning came from a camp across the river. So the realm visits.
2. **Send the column** - tell Zo you are ready and he gives the order: a public march on the goblin camp, announced to the whole realm. (Not ready? Eat something, sharpen something, and come back.) If the Knight-Captain's column is already out somewhere else, Zo points you at that one instead - a march is a march. He'll warn you if it is fighting on wilderness ground.
3. **Fight beside the Knights of Lumbridge** - type `::march` and you are sent to the column wherever it is. Stay in the battle line: the quest needs you to have a **real share of the fighting**, and the column must **win**. Ten knights alone get driven back more often than Zo likes to admit - when you don't march with them, they die.
   - Driven back? Speak with Zo and go again. Now you know what didn't work.
   - Won without you? Zo will tell you a march isn't something you watch from the courtyard. Go again - in the line this time.
4. **Report to General Zo** - the field is won. For now: goblins breed faster than the realm marches. That is the March - free, frequent, and it never waits for anyone. You will hear the muster call every half hour for as long as you are a soldier of the realm, and now you know what it is asking. You don't have to answer every one. But the ones you skip, the knights walk alone.

Type `::firstmarch` for your current objective, or open the Quest Journal with `::quests` (the quest also shows in the stock quest tab).

## What you learn

- The **March** is the realm's public offensive: automatic, supply-free, every ~30 minutes, and open to everyone. `::march` rallies you to it; `::marches` shows the board of hostile ground the Knight-Captain strikes.
- The realm **pays its soldiers**: everyone who fought shares the pooled spoils, scaled by how much of the fighting they did. See [Marches and the reconquest](/wiki/marches-and-the-reconquest).
- Marches can **fail**. The knights need you more than you need them.

## Rewards

1 Quest Point · 25 War Effort · your fighter's share of the march's spoils (auto-banked) · main campaign progression.
