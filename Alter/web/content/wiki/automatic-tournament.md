The **Automatic Tournament** is the server's scheduled knockout bracket: a broadcast goes out, the
brave type `::tournament`, and a field of fighters is cut down round by round until one champion
takes the purse. It's pure PvP on a level field - **everyone fights in the same kit**, at their own
real stats, and **nothing anyone owns is ever at risk**.

## How it runs

1. **Signup opens** - announced server-wide on a regular schedule. Type `::tournament` from any
   safe zone to enter; you're teleported to the arena lobby while the field forms.
2. **The bracket begins** - entrants are shuffled and paired. An odd fighter out draws a **bye**
   straight into the next round.
3. **Every match is a real duel** - each pair is teleported into their **own private copy of the
   arena pit** (all matches of a round run at once), dressed in the **standard tournament kit** -
   the NH tribrid set: whip, mage and range switches, claws spec, brews and veng runes. A
   3... 2... 1... countdown, then it's live. No outside interference, no teleporting out, no
   holding hands with the fog - just the two of you.
4. **Losing** - die, log out, or hold **less health when a stalled match times out** - knocks you
   out of the bracket: your own gear is returned instantly and you're sent back to where you
   signed up. Winners return to the lobby for the next round.
5. **The champion** takes a **coin purse that scales with the field** - the more who enter, the
   fatter the prize.

## The level playing field

- **Same kit for everyone.** Your bank buys you nothing here; the tournament hands both fighters
  the identical loadout. Wins come from switches, spec timing and nerve.
- **Your real levels.** Unlike LMS there's no stat standardization - a maxed main hits like one.
- **Zero risk.** Your own inventory and equipment are stashed before each match and restored the
  moment it ends - win, lose, disconnect, even a server crash mid-fight restores your gear on your
  next login. The loaner kit can't be kept, banked or traded.

## Odds and ends

- Minimum 2 entrants, capped at 32 - if too few sign up, the tournament is called off.
- Wander into the wilderness or into other content between rounds and you're **withdrawn** - the
  bracket won't teleport you out of a fight you started elsewhere.
- The purse pays to your inventory, overflowing to your bank.
