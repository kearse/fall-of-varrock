The **Kalphite Queen** is the desert's two-phase boss, kept exactly as you remember her.

## Getting there

Open the **teleport portal → Bosses → Kalphite Queen**. You land in her chamber. Multi-way
combat.

## The fight

**Form 1 (flying, 255 HP)** — heavily armoured against stab, slash, ranged and magic; use a
**crush** weapon. She melees when you stand next to her (max 31), fires a **ranged** volley that
hits everyone in the chamber, and a **magic** bolt that **chains** between players standing
within two tiles of each other — spread out in a team.

When form 1 dies she **transforms at full health** and is immune for a few seconds.

**Form 2 (crawling, 255 HP)** — now armoured against melee and weak to **ranged and magic**.
Same three attacks, so keep switching overheads between Melee up close and Missiles/Magic at
range.

## Loot

Form 2 pays out: the classic KQ table (rune chainbody, lava battlestaff, seeds, potions, noted
resources, elite clues) with the chase uniques below, all **Collection Log** entries:

| Drop | Rarity |
|---|---|
| Dragon chainbody | 1/128 |
| Dragon 2h sword | 1/128 |
| KQ head | 1/128 |
| Jar of sand | 1/128 |
| Kalphite princess (pet) | 1/1000 |

Kills pay **25 Boss Tickets** and count toward `::kc`. She rises again about 30 seconds after
a kill.
