**Moons of Peril** is the mid-level boss dungeon from Old School, kept to its original loop:
three imprisoned Moons, each fought alone in its own chamber, and a **Lunar Chest** whose
reward grows with how many Moons you've subdued this run.

## Getting there

Open the **teleport portal → Mini-Games → Moons of Peril** (or `::moons`). You arrive in the
antechamber. The Blood Moon's chamber is to the **west**, the Eclipse Moon's to the **east**, the
Blue Moon's to the **north**; the Lunar Chest waits in the **Ancient Shrine** to the east.
Camps in each cavern have a **cooking stove** (Make-cuppa restores your run energy) and
**supply crates** that hand out a bundle of cooked bream every few minutes.

Walk through a chamber's doorway to begin — you get your own copy of the chamber. Walk back
through the doorway to leave.

## The fights

Every Moon has 500 hitpoints and the same bones: a **three-strike melee combo** every six ticks
(4, 8 and 20 — if a strike misses, the rest miss too) and **Eyatlalli's glyph**, a moonfire
marker that moves clockwise around the room every two attacks. **Stand on or beside the glyph**
during normal attacks or you take rapid damage. After six attacks the Moon uses one of its two
specials, alternating.

| Moon | Special 1 | Special 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Blood Moon** — every strike that lands **heals it** (the third for 30–50) | **Blood rain**: it vanishes and pools of blood spatter the floor. Stay off them. | **Blood jaguar**: a jaguar rises beside the glyph inside a square of pools. The Moon is immune until the jaguar dies; every pool you stand in heals it threefold. |
| **Blue Moon** | **Weapon freeze**: your weapon is torn off and sealed in ice at the glyph. Stand there a few ticks to shatter it, then re-equip. Icy spikes fall meanwhile. | **Frost storm**: both braziers at the chamber's ends go out. **Feed** them while two storms sweep the floor — each unlit brazier heals the Moon. |
| **Eclipse Moon** | **Searing rays**: a moon shield appears and shoves you behind it, then circles the room. Stay in its shadow. | **Clones**: you're bound at the centre while five waves of three clones strike. **Attack a clone before it strikes** to parry — the parry lands on the Moon. |

Subduing a Moon carries you back out of its door. Re-entering a chamber you have already
subdued this run spawns the **Enraged** Moon: harder, and worth nothing extra at the chest.

## The Lunar Chest

The chest **unlocks once you have subdued all three Moons** at least once. From then on, claim
it with any number subdued this run:

- **1 / 3 / 6** common rolls for **1 / 2 / 3** Moons (atlatl darts, blessed bone shards,
  wyrmling and sun-kissed bones, swamp tar, water orbs, supercompost, soft clay, harralander,
  irit, maple and yew seeds).
- For **each Moon subdued**, a **1 in 56** chance at one of that Moon's four pieces:
  - Blood Moon: helm, chestplate, tassets, **Dual macuahuitl**
  - Blue Moon: helm, chestplate, tassets, **Blue moon spear**
  - Eclipse Moon: helm, chestplate, tassets, **Eclipse atlatl**
- **10 Boss Tickets** per Moon.

Claiming re-locks the run. All twelve uniques are **Collection Log** entries; `::kc` tracks each
Moon and your chest count.
