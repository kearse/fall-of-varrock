Death works like real OSRS, **everywhere** - not just the wilderness. Dying in town, on a road, or to a monster costs you items under the same rules as a PvP death.

## What you keep

| Status | Items kept |
| --- | --- |
| Unskulled | 3 most valuable |
| Unskulled + Protect Item prayer | 4 |
| Skulled | 0 |
| Skulled + Protect Item | 1 |

Untradeables are always kept and don't use up a slot. Check exactly what you'd keep and lose right now - including your total risk value - with `::kod` (Items Kept on Death).

## Where your items go

- **Killed by a player in the wilderness** - your loot seals into a [loot key](/wiki/wilderness-loot-keys) owned by your killer.
- **Died anywhere else** (monsters, roads, bot ambushes outside the wild) - your items drop into a **reclaim pile on your death tile that only you can see for ~15 minutes**. Get back there and pick your things up. After that grace period the pile turns public for a few minutes, then despawns.

## Safe deaths

Some content never drops items on death:

- The **Fight Cave** wave arena (`::arena`)
- The **Zulrah** shrine (`::zulrah`)
- Instanced content (Wizard Tower runs, raid instances)

Everywhere else, assume full death rules apply - including your unclaimed loot keys, which are **always** destroyed on death regardless of protection.
