The **Fight Cave** has returned, rebuilt on the real cave's wave engine. Fight escalating
waves of TzHaar in a **private instance of the actual cave**, ending in **TzTok-Jad** —
the prayer-switching final exam of old-school PvM. Survive him and the **Fire cape** is
yours.

## Getting there

Teleport portal → **Minigames → Fight Cave** lands you beside **TzHaar-Mej-Jal** at the
cave entrance. Talk to him (or `::arena`) to start a run; `::leave` flees; dying is a
**safe death** — the cave keeps no corpses.

## The run

A run is **waves 50 to 63** of the authentic escalation - the same monster mix the real
cave produces, spawning from the cave's classic corner points in a rotating pattern:

| Monster | Threat |
|---|---|
| Tz-Kih (the bats) | Drain your **Prayer** every attack - kill them first |
| Tz-Kek (the blobs) | Melee; recoil damage; **split in two** when killed |
| Tok-Xil (the rangers) | Accurate ranged from anywhere - Protect from Missiles |
| Yt-MejKot (the meleers) | Big melee; **heal themselves and allies** - drag them away |
| Ket-Zek (the fire mages) | 49s of magic - Protect from Magic |
| **TzTok-Jad** (wave 63) | See below |

Waves **14, 30 and 62** in the classic table are the doubled "orange" waves — you'll meet
wave 62's **two Ket-Zeks** right before Jad.

## TzTok-Jad

Jad hits through the ceiling (max 97) but **telegraphs every attack**:

- **Front-leg slam** → RANGED — Protect from Missiles
- **Rearing fire-breath** → MAGIC — Protect from Magic
- Up close he can also melee — stand off him and watch the stance.

The protection check happens **at impact**, a beat after the animation — switching on the
telegraph works, exactly as it should. At **half health four Yt-HurKot healers** swarm to
him; tag each one to drag it off or he heals back to full.

## Rewards

- **Fire cape** on every clear (tradeable — clears supply the market).
- **TokKul** for every run: the classic per-wave payout, +4,000 bonus on a clear.
- **Boss Tickets**: a big bundle for the clear, a per-wave consolation if you fall.
- **TzRek-Jad** — 1/1000 per clear, Collection Log entry.
- Your **best wave** is remembered.

## Practice mode

Mej-Jal will run you **any single wave, 1-63**, no rewards — the classic way to learn
Jad's stances (`::jad` jumps straight to him). Practice costs nothing but supplies.
