The economy centres on the **Lumbridge courtyard market** — every essential vendor stands in two shop rows by the castle, so you never hunt for a shop.

## The Trading Post

`::market` opens the **Trading Post** — a player marketplace with an NPC backstop so trading works even at low population:

- **Buying:** pay full item value.
- **Selling:** receive **85%** of value (the 15% margin is a deliberate gold sink).
- **Always in stock:** smithing feedstock (coal, gold/mithril/adamantite bars), high runes (nature, law, death, blood, cosmic, astral), essence, uncut gems, big bones, high herbs (ranarr, snapdragon, torstol, kwuarm), raw sharks and manta rays.
- Items other players sell get listed for anyone to buy back.

**Bonds are never bought by the post** — they only move player-to-player. See [Bonds & membership](/wiki/bonds-and-membership).

## The market rows

- **General stores & supply vendors** — food, tools, runes, gear up to the **adamant cap** (dragon and rune-plus come from drops, the [Forge](/wiki/the-forge) and war loot; better food and potions are gated behind skilling).
- **Armoury** — heavier armour unlocks with your [feudal rank](/wiki/titles-and-citizenship).
- **Loot Chest** — claim your [wilderness loot keys](/wiki/wilderness-loot-keys).
- **Emblem Trader** — the [PK Rewards shop](/wiki/pk-rewards-and-stats) (Blood Money).
- **Bond Merchant** — redeem [bonds](/wiki/bonds-and-membership).
- **Gambling Host** — [house dice](/wiki/gambling) at the south end.
- **Reward shops** — spend **Boss tickets** and **Vote tickets** (tradeable, stackable ticket items earned from bossing and voting).
- **The fountain altars** — at the north end of the aisle: the **Altar of the Occult** (west of the fountain) [switches your spellbook](/wiki/unlocking-spellbooks), and a **prayer altar** (east of the fountain) recharges prayer points — bones offered on it earn boosted Prayer xp.

## Player trading

Direct player-to-player trading works everywhere with the standard two-screen trade interface. Check your net worth with `::wealth`, or see the richest players online with `::wealthtop`.
