The economy centres on the **Lumbridge courtyard market** - every essential vendor stands in two shop rows by the castle, so you never hunt for a shop. This page explains how the market works; for the full stock-and-price reference see the [shops directory](/wiki/shops-directory), and for the route to every rare see [Path to end game](/wiki/path-to-end-game).

## Shops open instantly - no dialogue

Clicking **Trade** (or Talk-to on a pure shopkeeper) opens the store **directly** - no chat menus. Vendors that run several stores show a **tab strip along the bottom of the shop window**; click a tab to switch store instantly:

- **Quartermaster** - the Barrows wing (coins); Talk-to takes war-supply hand-ins.
- **Emblem Trader** - Supplies / Spec weapons / Wildy sets / Revenant tabs.
- **Melee Shop (Horvik)** - Weapons / Rank armour tabs.
- **Herblore Shop (Jatix)** - all four seed stores, herbs, secondaries, unfinished potions and farm tools as tabs.
- **Skilling, Fishing and Rewards shops** - Tools/Materials, Supplies/Fish and the Vote rewards tab.

The odd vendor with something to *say* still talks on **Talk-to** (Zaff can still lift you to the rune altar; the Quartermaster still takes war-supply hand-ins) - but **Trade always goes straight to the wares**.

## Selling to shops

Every **coin shop buys back what it stocks at 70% of value** - with the shop window open, right-click an item in your inventory and pick **Sell 1/5/10** (or Sell X). Runes go back to Zaff, fish to Gerrant, herbs and seeds to Jatix, armour to the Apprentice, and so on. The **general store** by the bridge buys **odds and ends** (anything up to 500 gp value) at the same 70%, and the **Trading Post** (`::market`) buys **everyday commodities** - runes, ores, bars, logs, food, herbs, gems, essence, planks - also at 70%. Reward and ticket shops (Quartermaster, Emblem Trader, Valaine, the currency desks) never buy back, and nothing they sell can be sold to *any* NPC.

**Every NPC pays the same 70%, and no NPC buys gear or crafted goods.** Smithed armour, fletched bows, cut gems, finished potions and every piece of boss or PvP equipment are sold to *players*: list them on the [Grand Exchange](#the-grand-exchange), where commodities have an NPC floor at 70% and everything else floats free - the margin comes from a real buyer, not the coin printer.

## The Trading Post

`::market` opens the **Trading Post** - a player marketplace with an NPC backstop so trading works even at low population:

- **Buying:** pay full item value.
- **Selling:** receive **70%** of value (the same rate as every shop; the margin is a deliberate gold sink).
- **Nothing is house stock:** everything on the shelf is something a **player sold**, waiting to be bought back - if the shelf is empty, nobody has sold anything yet.
- It buys **everyday commodities only** - the same list the Grand Exchange backs (runes, ores, bars, logs, food, herbs, gems, essence, planks). Gear, crafted goods and anything from a reward shelf go to the [Grand Exchange](#the-grand-exchange) for other players to buy.

**Bonds are never bought by the post** - they only move player-to-player. See [Bonds & membership](/wiki/bonds-and-membership).

## The Grand Exchange

The Trading Post is growing into a full **Grand Exchange** - the classic OSRS offer book, where you post buy and sell orders for coins and they fill against other players. It's being wired up now; here's how it works:

- **Post an offer:** pick an item (the same search box you already know), set a quantity and a price per item, and confirm. Buy offers hold your coins, sell offers hold your items - both wait safely until they fill or you cancel, then the proceeds land in your collection box.
- **Stores set the minimums:** everyday commodities (runes, bars, ores, logs, food, herbs, gems, essence, planks) have an NPC **floor** - a sell offer at or below **70%** of value fills instantly, so a skiller can always cash out. Only the boring necessities the shops sell without limit (runes, arrows, cooked food, planks) also have an NPC **ceiling** at **100%** of value, so magic ammo and food are never blocked by an empty exchange. Raw materials, gear, megarares and the special currencies have **no** NPC seller: their price floats on what players will pay.
- **Special currencies trade here too:** Blood Money and Vote Tickets are tradeable items you can sell for coins - a PKer can cash out Blood Money to a PvMer, a voter can sell tickets - so gold and grind flow between playstyles. No NPC sells either currency: their gp value is whatever players pay here. (Boss Tickets were retired in September 2026 - bosses pay in drops.)

## The market rows

- **General stores & supply vendors** - food, tools, runes, gear up to the **adamant cap** (dragon and rune-plus come from drops, the [Forge](/wiki/the-forge) and war loot; better food and potions are gated behind skilling).
- **Armoury** - heavier armour unlocks with your [feudal rank](/wiki/titles-and-citizenship).
- **Loot Chest** - claim your [wilderness loot keys](/wiki/wilderness-loot-keys).
- **Emblem Trader** - the [PK Rewards vendor](/wiki/pk-rewards-and-stats) (Blood Money): PK supplies, spec weapons, the wilderness sets (Vesta's/Statius's/Morrigan's/Zuriel's) and revenant weapons. The PvP gear chase lives here.
- **Quartermaster - the Warlord's Armoury** - the **Barrows wing** for coins (the mid-game gp sink) and the war-supply hand-in. The old Boss-Ticket catalogue is retired: chase gear comes from the bosses and the [war](/wiki/the-war-explained) that drop it, and moves only between players. War-forged Torva/Masori/Ancestral are never sold: the Smith makes them, nobody else.
- **Bond Merchant** - redeem [bonds](/wiki/bonds-and-membership).
- **Donor Store** (`::donorstore`) - cosmetic rares (partyhats, santa, h'ween masks) for **Donor Points**; tradeable, stat-less flex.
- **Commander's Regalia** (`::prestigeshop`) - the sanguine regalia set for **Prestige** (commanders only earn it).
- **Gambling Host** - [house dice](/wiki/gambling) at the south end.
- **Reward shop** - spend **Vote tickets** (a tradeable, stackable ticket item earned from voting).
- **The Grand Exchange stand** - on the old fountain site at the north end of the aisle: click it to open the Exchange (the clerk and `::ge` work too). The **Altar of the Occult** now stands on the north exit path just past the teleport portal and [switches your spellbook](/wiki/unlocking-spellbooks); the church altar just east of the market recharges prayer points - bones offered on it earn boosted Prayer xp.

## Player trading

Direct player-to-player trading works everywhere with the standard two-screen trade interface. Check your net worth with `::wealth`, or see the richest players online with `::wealthtop`.
