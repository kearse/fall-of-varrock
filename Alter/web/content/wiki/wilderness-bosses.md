The **Wilderness bosses** are back at the lairs every RSPS veteran remembers — the old surface
spots, all **multi-way**, all inside the **Wilderness**: other players can attack you, and you
drop your items on death like anywhere else in the Wild.

## Getting there

Open the **teleport portal → Bosses** and pick the boss; you land just **outside its aggro
range**, so you get a breath before it notices you. The Wilderness level is shown on each row.

**Scorpia's cave** is Wilderness level 54 (PvP, skulls and item drops apply underground exactly
as on the surface). Three **cavern mouths** on the surface north of the lava maze (around
3231,3936 / 3241,3949 / 3231,3951) lead down; the matching **crevices** inside the cave lead
back up — or the portal drops you straight in. Her **offspring** roam the cave and are
aggressive, with a light poison.

When a boss dies, everyone at the lair gets a **respawn countdown** ("Scorpia will respawn in
30 seconds… in 10 seconds… has respawned").

**Callisto** lives in **Callisto's Den**: the portal drops you by the **Cave Entrance** just
south of the Demonic Ruins (level 41). **Enter** it to climb down (no fee, no diary — **Peek**
tells you whether the bear is up and how many players are already inside); the **Cave** at the
south end of the den climbs back out. The den is multi-way and still the Wilderness.

## The bosses

| Boss | Wild lvl | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Callisto** (lvl 470) | 41 | Melee up close (max 60), a **shockwave** at range that always hits, and a **roar** that throws you back, stuns you and **strips your prayers**. Heals himself now and then. |
| **Vet'ion** (lvl 454) | 34 | Melee (max 45), an unblockable **earthquake** to everyone near him, and **lightning** on three tiles around you. Below half health he calls two **skeleton hellhounds** — he is **immune until they die**. Kill him and he **rises again** at full health with greater hounds; only the reborn form drops loot. |
| **Venenatis** (lvl 464) | 28 | Melee within two tiles (max 50) or a **magic** volley on everyone nearby — when the volley connects it can **sap 10–19 prayer points**; a **web** that pins you (50 through prayer); poison. |
| **Scorpia** (lvl 225) | 54 (cave) | Melee (max 16) with heavy **poison** (20). Below 100 health two **guardians** appear and heal her until you kill them. |
| **Chaos Elemental** (lvl 305) | 51 | A tri-style bolt (magic / ranged / melee — pray by feel), a **teleport** that throws you 1–4 tiles, and a **disarm** that pulls a worn item into your inventory. |
| **Chaos Fanatic** (lvl 202) | 41 | Fast magic bolts (2-tick), a green **three-tile barrage** (30 through prayer) and the same disarm trick. Ranting the whole time. |
| **Crazy Archaeologist** (lvl 204) | 23 | Ranged and melee (max 15) plus **"Rain of knowledge!"** — books fall on your tile and around it, then again where you stood (24 each through prayer). Step away. |

## Loot

Every boss rolls its classic table (rune pickaxes and 2h swords, runes, seeds, noted supplies,
dark crabs, super restores, clue scrolls). The chase drops, all **Collection Log** entries:

| Boss | Uniques | Rarity |
|---|---|---|
| Callisto | Tyrannical ring · Dragon pickaxe · Callisto cub | 1/512 · 1/256 · 1/1000 |
| Vet'ion | Ring of the gods · Dragon pickaxe · Vet'ion jr. | 1/512 · 1/256 · 1/1000 |
| Venenatis | Treasonous ring · Dragon pickaxe · Venenatis spiderling | 1/512 · 1/256 · 1/1000 |
| Scorpia | Odium shard 3 · Malediction shard 3 · Scorpia's offspring | 1/256 · 1/256 · 1/1000 |
| Chaos Elemental | Dragon pickaxe · Pet chaos elemental | 1/256 · 1/300 |
| Chaos Fanatic | Odium shard 1 · Malediction shard 1 | 1/256 each |
| Crazy Archaeologist | Fedora · Odium shard 2 · Malediction shard 2 | 1/128 · 1/256 · 1/256 |

Combine the three Odium shards into the **Odium ward** and the three Malediction shards into
the **Malediction ward**. Kills count toward `::kc`.
