The **teleport portal** at Lumbridge home is the fastest way anywhere. It's organised into tabbed categories, every destination carries a **danger tag**, and it remembers where you've been.

## Commands

- `::portal` - overview: categories, your recent teleports, what's popular right now
- `::portal <category>` - list that category's destinations with danger tags
- `::portalto <key>` - teleport by key (e.g. `::portalto skill_mining`)
- `::portalback` - repeat your last teleport

## The tabs

| Tab | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| Basics | Home, market, prayer altar |
| Skilling | The Mire hub + a stop for every skill |
| War | Varrock raid, frontiers, Goblin Warren, Recruit Trials |
| Bosses | **Corp Beast (Event)** - the [war world boss](/wiki/corporeal-beast). The retired [roster](/wiki/bosses-overview) shows greyed out as **coming soon**, lighting up as each rebuilt fight returns |
| Wilderness | The [bot zones](/wiki/pk-bots-and-highwaymen) by depth, level 5 → 55 |
| Slayer | Vannaka and slayer content |
| Minigames | [Wizard Tower](/wiki/wizard-tower) - the rest of the tab is greyed-out **coming soon** entries |
| Events | Coming soon |
| Donator | Coming soon |

## Danger tags - read them

- **SAFE** - no PvP, no risk
- **HOSTILE** - dangerous monsters, no PvP
- **WILD (level N)** - wilderness: full PvP, [loot keys](/wiki/wilderness-loot-keys), and your [death rules](/wiki/death-and-item-protection) apply

The portal will happily drop you at level-55 wilderness with a bank-value inventory. The tag is the only warning you get - check it before you click.

The world boss also has a direct command - `::worldboss` - that works from anywhere. Remember **Tele Block** exists: a blocked player can't portal out for 5 minutes.
