The **Dagannoth Kings** share one multi-way chamber at the bottom of Waterbirth Dungeon, ringed
by **spinolyps** that poison and sap prayer. Each king uses one style and is near-immune to
the two he doesn't use.

## Getting there

Open the **teleport portal → Bosses → Dagannoth Kings**. You land at the chamber's edge.

## The kings

| King | Attacks with | Weak to | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dagannoth Rex** | Melee (max 26) | **Magic** | Trap him behind the spinolyp rocks and cast from range. |
| **Dagannoth Prime** | Magic (max 50) | **Ranged** | His bolt **splashes** onto anyone standing next to his target. |
| **Dagannoth Supreme** | Ranged (max 30) | **Melee** | His volley hits **everyone within six tiles** of him. |

The **spinolyps** around the water are stationary: their ranged spit can **poison** and their
magic spit **drains prayer**. Kill or avoid them.

## Loot

Every king drops **dagannoth bones and hide** plus a roll on his own classic table. The chase
uniques, all **Collection Log** entries, at Old School odds:

| King | Uniques (1/128 each) |
|---|---|
| Rex | Berserker ring, Warrior ring, Dragon axe |
| Prime | Seers ring, Mud battlestaff, Dragon axe |
| Supreme | Archers ring, Seercull, Dragon axe |

Each king also has a **1/1000** pet. Kills pay **15 Boss Tickets** per king and count toward
`::kc` individually.
