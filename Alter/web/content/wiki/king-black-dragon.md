The **King Black Dragon** is back at its lair, fought the Old School way: a shared, multi-way
arena where dragonfire protection is the whole fight.

## Getting there

Open the **teleport portal → Bosses → King Black Dragon**. You land inside the lair island; the
dragon roams the middle. It is **multi-way combat** — bring friends or expect company.

## The fight

Up close it bites (max 25). At range it breathes one of **four dragonfires**:

- **Fire** — plain dragonfire, up to 65 unmitigated.
- **Ice** — can **freeze** you in place.
- **Shock** — can **drain** Attack, Strength, Defence, Ranged and Magic.
- **Poison** — can **poison** you.

An antifire potion plus an anti-dragon shield (or dragonfire shield/ward) reduces the plain
breath to nothing; the three special breaths still hit for a little (10–12) through full
protection, exactly like Old School. Protect from Magic alone only softens them.

## Loot

Every kill drops **dragon bones** and **2 black dragonhide** plus a roll on the classic KBD
table (rune longsword, adamant armour, dragon med helm, runes, dragon dart/arrow tips, noted
supplies, elite clues). Chase drops, all **Collection Log** entries:

| Drop | Rarity |
|---|---|
| KBD heads | 1/128 |
| Dragon pickaxe | 1/1000 |
| Draconic visage | 1/1000 |
| Prince black dragon (pet) | 1/1000 |

Kills pay **20 Boss Tickets** and count toward `::kc`.
