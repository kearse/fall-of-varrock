We build in the open, so you'll see some destinations and menus marked **coming soon** in-game. This page keeps an honest line between what's live and what's next.

## Live today

Everything else on this wiki is playable now: the War ([public marches](/wiki/marches-and-the-reconquest) every half hour that anyone can join, Lord operations, campaigns, conquests, the [Corporeal Beast world boss](/wiki/corporeal-beast), troops), [ranks earned by coin and service](/wiki/titles-and-citizenship), the optional [Rogue Knight ladder](/wiki/rogue-knights), the [Wizard Tower](/wiki/wizard-tower) and the spellbook unlock, [duelling and staking](/wiki/duel-arena-staking) (challenge anyone in a safe zone), loot keys, PK bots, [companions](/wiki/companions) (one at your side, a roster that grows with rank), the Mire skilling hub, the Forge, Trading Post, bonds and membership.

## The September foundation

The world just moved onto its long-term footing: Lumbridge is never besieged, **Varrock stays fallen** (it is the war's permanent objective, not a city to liberate), Falador and Al Kharid are ordinary safe towns, and the War now runs on two clearly separate meters - your lifetime **War Effort** and the realm's shared **Realm Supplies** ([Supplying the war](/wiki/supplying-the-war)). The story quests that build on this land next.

## The retired roster - returning rebuilt

The hand-built boss lairs and minigames (KBD, Zulrah, Barrows, God Wars, the wilderness bosses, the elite tier, the Fight Cave and Inferno, Castle Wars, Last Man Standing and the rest) have been **temporarily retired**: they weren't accurate enough, and half-right fights aren't worth your grind. Each one is being rebuilt properly and will return **one at a time** as a finished fight - the greyed-out portal entries light up as they land, each with a news post and a wiki page. The Corporeal Beast war event and the Wizard Tower carry the flag meanwhile.

## In the works

- **The returning bosses and minigames** - see above; the rebuilt roster is the top of the queue.
- **Raids** - **Nightmare**, **Theatre of Blood** and **Chambers of Xeric** are the planned lineup, on the same properly-built standard.
- **Events tab** - HP events, tournaments, treasure hunts, vote bosses.
- **Donator zones** - dedicated PvM and skilling areas for members.
- **More PvP zones** - Fun-PK, Risk Zone, Edge bridding, Mage Bank and Ferox-style safety are portal placeholders.
- **The story quests** - The Last Free City and the chapters after it: a guided road through the war that starts marches, checks that you really fought, and opens new ground as you go. The quest framework is in; the quests are being written.
- **The war marches on** - Varrock is the permanent front; more march targets (the Port Sarim docks, the Wild Bandit Camp) and more fronts grow from there.

## How updates land

New features ship with a news post, a Discord announcement, and a wiki page the same day. If a "coming soon" tag disappears in-game, the wiki article for it is already live.
