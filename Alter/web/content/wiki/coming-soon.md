We build in the open, so you'll see some destinations and menus marked **coming soon** in-game. This page keeps an honest line between what's live and what's next.

## Live today

Everything else on this wiki is playable now: the War (campaigns, conquests, the [Corporeal Beast world boss](/wiki/corporeal-beast), troops), the [Rogue Knight ladder](/wiki/rogue-knights), the [Wizard Tower](/wiki/wizard-tower) and the spellbook unlock, loot keys, PK bots, companions, the Mire skilling hub, the Forge, Trading Post, bonds and membership.

## The retired roster - returning rebuilt

The hand-built boss lairs and minigames (KBD, Zulrah, Barrows, God Wars, the wilderness bosses, the elite tier, the Fight Cave and Inferno, Castle Wars, the Duel Arena, Last Man Standing and the rest) have been **temporarily retired**: they weren't accurate enough, and half-right fights aren't worth your grind. Each one is being rebuilt properly and will return **one at a time** as a finished fight - the greyed-out portal entries light up as they land, each with a news post and a wiki page. The Corporeal Beast war event and the Wizard Tower carry the flag meanwhile.

## In the works

- **The returning bosses and minigames** - see above; the rebuilt roster is the top of the queue.
- **Raids** - **Nightmare**, **Theatre of Blood** and **Chambers of Xeric** are the planned lineup, on the same properly-built standard.
- **Events tab** - HP events, tournaments, treasure hunts, vote bosses.
- **Donator zones** - dedicated PvM and skilling areas for members.
- **More PvP zones** - Fun-PK, Risk Zone, Edge bridding, Mage Bank and Ferox-style safety are portal placeholders.
- **The war marches on** - Varrock is the first hostile city; the campaign map grows from there.

## How updates land

New features ship with a news post, a Discord announcement, and a wiki page the same day. If a "coming soon" tag disappears in-game, the wiki article for it is already live.
