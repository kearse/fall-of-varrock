The **Royal Smith** in the Lumbridge castle courtyard makes the best gear in the game - and no single grind produces it. Every war-forged piece takes the whole realm: a boss-tier base item, your **Commendations** from the war, runite bars from the smelters, and a coin fee. Not to be confused with [the Forge](/wiki/the-forge), the Mire's rune→dragon anvil.

## The recipes

Forging requires the rank of **Knight** (the finished pieces are Lord-tier to wear - the [rank ladder](/wiki/titles-and-citizenship) stays king). Every successful forging is announced across the realm.

**Body & legs** - 25 Commendations + 20 runite bars + 250,000 coins each:

| You bring | The Smith returns |
| --- | --- |
| Bandos chestplate | **Torva platebody** |
| Bandos tassets | **Torva platelegs** |
| Armadyl chestplate | **Masori body** |
| Armadyl chainskirt | **Masori chaps** |
| Ahrim's robetop | **Ancestral robe top** |
| Ahrim's robeskirt | **Ancestral robe bottom** |

**The helm tier** - 15 Commendations + **1 Warden's ember** + 10 runite bars + 150,000 coins:

| You bring | The Smith returns |
| --- | --- |
| Neitiznot faceguard | **Torva full helm** |
| Armadyl helmet | **Masori mask** |
| Ahrim's hood | **Ancestral hat** |

## Where the ingredients come from

- **Base items** - boss drops and deep-wilderness [PK-bot loot keys](/wiki/pk-bots-and-highwaymen). Tradeable: buy them if you'd rather.
- **Commendations** - paid on every WON [march, campaign or conquest](/wiki/marches-and-the-reconquest), scaled by your contribution. **Untradeable** - the one part of max gear no coin can buy.
- **Warden's embers** - the [Grand March's](/wiki/marches-and-the-reconquest) prize: guaranteed for the Warden's top damage dealer, rolled for every other fighter. Tradeable.
- **Runite bars** - smelt them, or buy them off the skillers filling [supply contracts](/wiki/slayer-and-contracts).
- **Coins** - the fee is the sink that keeps the economy honest.

For where every base item and every other rare comes from, see [Path to end game](/wiki/path-to-end-game).

## Can I just buy a finished piece?

Yes. War-forged gear is **fully tradeable** - a rich player can buy a Torva platebody off the grinder who made it, exactly like any drop. What can't happen is gear appearing from nowhere: every forged piece in the economy represents real marches fought by somebody. As the Smith puts it: *"Sell it if you like. Somebody marched for it either way."*
