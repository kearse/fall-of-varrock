**Last Man Standing (LMS)** is the authentic OSRS battle royale. You bring **nothing** of your own -
your gear is set aside for the round and you fight in the **kit you've built with Lisa**. Everyone
drops onto an instanced copy of the LMS island **at the same combat stats**, loots the village
**chests** for upgrades, and fights free-for-all while a **fog** closes in and herds the survivors
together. The **last competitor standing wins**.

This is a separate minigame from the [PK Training Arena](/wiki/pk-training-arena) - that one is a
safe sparring ground; this one is a real fight to be the last alive.

## Getting in

Talk to **Lisa** at the LMS lobby in Lumbridge, or use `::lms` to teleport straight to her, and
choose **"Join a game."** You'll wait in the lobby for a few seconds while the field fills, then the
game begins.

Short on players? Lisa fills the remaining slots with **competitors** so a game always runs - even
if you're the only person queued. You'll have to fight your way to the top of a full field of up to
**eight**.

## Build your kit

Pick **"Build my kit"** at Lisa and the **visual kit editor** opens: the category options sit on
item tabs (click one to pick it - your current pick glows gold), and the equipment doll and
inventory show a **live preview of exactly what you'll spawn with**, real item icons and all. Your
picks save the moment you click them and are **kept between games** - set it once, tweak it any
time:

- **Armour style** - Melee (Neitiznot/Fighter torso/Defender), Ranged (black d'hide), Magic
  (Mystic/Occult), or Hybrid (d'hide worn, mystic switches carried).
- **Main weapon** - Abyssal whip, Magic shortbow (with rune arrows), or Ancient staff.
- **Spec weapon** - Dragon dagger or Granite maul.
- **Magic pack** - Ice mage (Ancient book + barrage runes), Vengeance (Lunar book + veng runes), or none.
- **Food plan** - Brews & restores, or straight sharks.

## How a round works

1. **You spawn wearing your built kit** - nothing you walked in with comes onto the island (it's
   stashed and returned when you leave). Everyone fights at the **same standardized combat stats**
   (99s with 52 Prayer), so rounds are decided by skill, not levels.
2. **Loot the chests.** The island's village houses are full of chests holding upgrades - whips,
   bows, staves, armour, brews, ammo, and the occasional **dragon claws** or **Armadyl godsword**.
   Each chest pays out once per game.
3. **Fight everyone.** It's free-for-all: every other competitor is a target.
4. **Beat the fog.** A safe zone shrinks toward the middle of the island over the round. Anyone
   caught **outside the fog takes damage every tick** - keep moving inward. Eliminating a competitor
   drops a small supply pile to you.
5. **Be the last alive** to win.

## Rewards

- **Win:** a large lump of **LMS points**.
- **Each kill:** a smaller amount of LMS points, plus a supply drop.

Spend LMS points at **Lisa's reward shop** (talk to her → **"Show me the rewards."**). Stock is **consumables and the classic LMS cosmetic halos** only - the strong weapons (granite maul, dragon claws, Armadyl godsword) come from the island chests **during a match**, not the shop.

## Maps

Games rotate between islands:

- **Deserted Island** - the ruined-village core.
- **The Ruins** - a tighter northern arena broken up by water.

## Good to know

- **You keep nothing and lose nothing.** Death in LMS is safe - your real gear is stored the moment
  you enter and restored the moment you leave, win or lose. Even a disconnect mid-game returns your
  gear on your next login.
- **Placement is shown** when you're knocked out (e.g. "You placed #4 of 8").
- **High Stakes mode** (wagered entry) is coming in a future update - for now every game is the
  points-only competitive mode.
