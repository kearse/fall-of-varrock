Guardians of the Rift plays as it does in OSRS, sized for our population: a game starts a
minute after the first player waits in the lobby, and the Great Guardian's charge scales with
the number of players.

## The round

1. **Lobby.** Wait south of the barrier; it drops when the game starts. Bank chest and the
   Rewards Guardian are here.
2. **Two minutes to prepare.** Mine **guardian remains** around the hall (any pickaxe; the
   large remains in the east need 56 Agility over the rubble, the huge remains in the west open
   through a portal every couple of minutes), craft the fragments into **guardian essence** at
   the workbench, grab **uncharged cells** (up to 10) and a **weak cell**.
3. **Portals.** From 160 seconds, and every 140 seconds after, one **elemental** and one
   **catalytic** Guardian opens for 100 seconds. Enter one you have the Runecraft level for:
   every guardian essence becomes that rune **and** a guardian stone, one carried uncharged
   cell is charged to the altar's tier, and you get the altar's Runecraft XP per essence.
4. **Power the Great Guardian** with your stones (Power-up). Each stone adds energy of its
   type to your score and raises the Guardian's power; the rift closes at **100%**.
5. **Hold the line.** Abyssal creatures pour from the rift after the two minutes. Use a
   charged cell on an inactive cell tile to raise a barrier (weak / medium / strong /
   overcharged); leeches slip through gaps and drain the Guardian, walkers batter barriers,
   guardians attack you. A broken tile costs **12 fragments** to repair. At **60% power** the
   temple rumbles, every barrier takes 50 damage and the mix turns nastier.

Dying inside is safe. Leaving through the barrier mid-game forfeits that game's energy.

## Scoring

At the close you earn **1 elemental point per 100 elemental energy** and **1 catalytic point
per 100 catalytic energy** (the remainder is a proportional chance of one more), and
**Runecraft XP equal to your level × 45** if you reached 300 energy. Points persist. `::gotr`
shows the state of play.

## Rewards

**Rewards Guardian** — one elemental plus one catalytic point per search (Big-search does
five; 25 abyssal pearls buy a search too): abyssal pearls, essence pouches you don't own yet,
runes (air to blood), talismans, intricate pouches; rarely Atlax's diary (1/20, once), a
catalytic talisman (1/200), the abyssal needle (1/300, once), the abyssal lantern (1/700), a
dye (1/1,200 each) or the **abyssal protector** pet (1/4,000). **Temple Supplies** (Apprentice
Felix) sells the **raiments of the eye** (hat 400, top 350, bottoms 350, boots 250 pearls), the
lantern (1,500), the needle (750) and talismans. Pearls are untradeable.
