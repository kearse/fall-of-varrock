**Vorkath** is the first boss of the rebuilt roster — an undead blue dragon fought **solo in
your own instance** on the island of Ungael. It is a fight about *reading attacks and moving*,
not just gear: two of its attacks must be dodged on foot, and its special phases punish
standing still.

## Getting there

Open the **teleport portal → Bosses → Vorkath**. You land on Ungael's surface; **climb over the
icy spines** to enter your private arena. Inside, Vorkath lies asleep in the crater —
**Poke** it when you're ready. Nobody else can enter your instance, and dying inside is a
**safe death** (you keep your items and wake up back home).

## The fight

Vorkath cycles **six regular attacks, then a special**, alternating between its two specials.

**Regular attacks** — protect against Missiles or Magic and watch for these:

- **Melee slash** when you stand next to it — range from a distance to avoid it entirely.
- **Ranged spike** and **magic blast** — moderate hits; overhead prayers block them.
- **Dragonfire, venom and pink dragonfire breath** — dragonfire mitigation applies
  (antifire potion + dragonfire shield/ward, or Protect from Magic). The **venom breath**
  can envenom you; the **pink breath strips your overhead prayers** — re-activate fast.
- **The high fireball** — Vorkath rears and launches a skull-fireball at your tile. **Move
  at least two tiles** before it lands or take up to 60 typeless damage.

**Special 1 — Zombified spawn (ice).** An icy barrage **freezes you in place** and Vorkath
becomes **immune to all damage**. A zombified spawn crawls toward you — kill it before it
reaches you (it has almost no defence and dies to anything) or it detonates for up to 60.

**Special 2 — Acid phase.** Vorkath floods the arena with acid pools and becomes
**half-damage resistant**, then spits **25 rapid fireballs** — each aimed at the tile you're
standing on, and each **misses entirely if you keep moving** (walk one tile side to side).
Standing in acid chips your health *and heals Vorkath* — stay off the green.

## Loot

Every kill drops **2 superior dragon bones** plus a solid coin/supply roll (wrath runes,
enchanted bolts, noted resources). The chase drops, all **Collection Log** entries:

| Drop | Rarity |
|---|---|
| Vorkath's head | 1/50 |
| Dragonbone necklace | 1/300 |
| Jar of decay | 1/500 |
| Skeletal visage | 1/500 |
| Vorki (pet) | 1/1000 |

Kills count toward `::kc`. After a kill, a fresh Vorkath
rises in the same instance — chain kills without re-entering.
