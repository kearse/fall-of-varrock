Varrock is never reclaimed — but it can be **worked**. Outside the Marches and Campaigns the
ruins are always open: the ambient dead, the four **captains**, and now a stronger layer for
players who want to live there.

Fallen Varrock is **PvP** everywhere except the west bank, the east bank and the Grand Exchange
pockets. Bring what you can lose.

## Elite undead

The streets north of the square and the palace approach are held by a stronger tier — **Fallen
Legionnaires, Grave Knights, Bone Warlocks, Plague Zombies, Drowned Marines, Wailing Shades and
Screaming Banshees**. Melee ones hit up to 28; the casters bolt from range, the Plague Zombie
poisons, the Shade and Banshee sap prayer. They drop supplies and **Varrock salvage**, with a
rare **Relic of old Varrock**.

## Salvage piles

Crates lie in the rubble of every district. **Search** one for 2–5 **Varrock salvage** and a 1/40
shot at a **relic**; the pile is bare for a minute and a half afterwards, and the noise sometimes
wakes an elite in the rubble. Salvage and relics are the **War-Forging** materials the Royal Smith
will take.

## Malachai the Hollow

Every twenty minutes or so a **hollow knight** rises at one of the district centres and the
whole world hears of it. He melees hard (max 30) and every sixth swing **wails**, sapping a fifth
of the prayer of everyone near him. He lingers ten minutes. Felling him drops **two relics**,
salvage and supplies, pays 15 Boss Tickets, 20 War Effort and 3 Commendations.

## The Palace Warden

The palace is the deep, repeatable target. **The Palace Warden** (900 HP, multi-way) melees for
30 and hurls **necrotic bolts** (24, drains prayer). Every eighth attack he **raises three dead**
beside you, and once below half health he calls a **Grave Chill** — a three-tick warning, then 20
through prayer to anyone still within two tiles. He drops **1–2 relics**, 12–20 salvage, dragon
bones and supplies, pays 30 Boss Tickets, 40 War Effort and 5 Commendations, and one kill in
eight yields a **Warden's ember**. He returns fifteen minutes after a kill.

## Arrav Intelligence

**Captain Rovin** stands in the west bank pocket. Knights and above (the Cursed Hero quest, once
it lands) can take one assignment at a time:

| Assignment | Work | Pay |
|---|---|---|
| Purge | 8–13 elite undead in a named district | 15 War Effort, 2 Commendations, 5 salvage |
| Salvage | 10–17 Varrock salvage from the piles | 12 War Effort, 2 Commendations, 1/6 relic |
| Bounty | Fell Malachai the Hollow | 25 War Effort, 4 Commendations, a relic |
| Warden | Fell the Palace Warden | 40 War Effort, 6 Commendations, 2 relics |

Assignments complete on their own as you work; `::intel` shows the one you hold. Relics and
embers are **Collection Log** entries; `::kc` tracks the Warden and Malachai.
