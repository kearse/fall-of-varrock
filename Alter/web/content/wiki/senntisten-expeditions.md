Beneath the Digsite lie the conduits of **Senntisten** — the buried Zarosian infrastructure
that amplified the Fall. An **expedition** is a private descent into its temple: clear the
wardens, face the Custodian, come back up with what the ruins give.

## Getting there

Open the **teleport portal → Mini-Games → Senntisten Expedition** and **Operate** one of the
Digsite winches (or `::expedition`). You're lowered into your own copy of the temple's western
corridor. You have **twelve minutes**; `::expedition` again hauls you up early. Dying inside is a
safe death.

## The run

1. **Wave 1 — the corridor.** Sentinels and a Buried Legionnaire (melee).
2. **Wave 2 — the hall.** Legionnaires, a Sentinel and two **Echoes of the Fall** (magic, sap
   prayer).
3. **Wave 3 — the hall.** More Legionnaires plus two **Conduit Warlocks** (magic bolts).
4. **The Custodian of Senntisten** — a Zarosian demon bound to the altar (700 HP). He melees
   for 28 and hurls necrotic bolts (26, drains prayer). Every seventh attack he charges a
   **conduit surge**: three ticks of warning, then 25 through prayer to anyone within two tiles —
   step away. Below half health he calls two Echoes.

Each wave cleared pays **2–4 Varrock salvage**.

## Rewards

The Custodian drops **2 Relics of old Varrock**, **10 salvage** and a **Senntisten expedition
log** page every kill, plus runes, bars, bones and restores, and pays **25 Boss Tickets, 30 War
Effort and 4 Commendations**. Relics and salvage are War-Forging materials; the log page is a
Collection Log chase. `::kc` tracks the Custodian; your completed expedition count is kept.

## Deep Senntisten

The great dungeon beyond the temple — harder wardens, rare rooms, original bosses and
higher-tier materials — opens behind *Beneath the Fallen Empire*. This is the door.
