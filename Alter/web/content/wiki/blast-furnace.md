The Blast Furnace works as it does on an OSRS "official" world: the dwarves keep the stove,
the pump and the pedals going, and you pay the coffer.

## How it works

1. **Pay the coffer** — Use the coffer (or Pay the Foreman). Minimum deposit 25,000 coins;
   the furnace draws **1,200 coins a minute (72,000 an hour)** while your ore is in the machine.
   Everyone pays. Withdraw what's left whenever you like.
2. **Put ore on the belt** — pick the ore, and all of it goes on with the coal it needs, taken
   from your pack or your **coal bag**. The machine holds 28 ores and 254 coal of yours at once.
   Coal here is **half** a normal furnace: steel 1, mithril 2, adamant 3, rune 4. Iron never
   fails.
3. **Take the bars** — the dwarves turn one bar every couple of seconds. Bars are hot: wear
   **ice gloves** (or smiths gloves) to take them straight out, or carry a **bucket of water**
   for each dispenser-load; bare hands get burned. The dispenser holds 28 bars of a type.
4. **Bank** at the chest and go again. `::bf` shows your coffer, belt and dispenser.

| Bar | Smithing | Coal | XP |

| Bronze | 1 | 0 | 6.2 |
| Iron | 15 | 0 | 12.5 |
| Silver | 20 | 0 | 13.7 |
| Steel | 30 | 1 | 17.5 |
| Gold | 40 | 0 | 22.5 (**56.2** with goldsmith gauntlets) |
| Mithril | 50 | 2 | 30 |
| Adamant | 70 | 3 | 37.5 |
| Rune | 85 | 4 | 50 |

The coal bag holds 27 coal (Fill / Empty / Check). Ore left on the belt when you log out is
sent to your bank.
