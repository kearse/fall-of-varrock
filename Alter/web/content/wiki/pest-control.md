Pest Control plays as it does in OSRS, sized for our population: a lander departs **one
minute after the first player boards** (no party minimum), and each game runs in its own
copy of the battleground.

## Landers

| Lander | Combat | Pest Control points per win |
|---|---|---|
| Novice | 40 | 10 |
| Intermediate | 70 | 15 |
| Veteran | 100 | 20 |

Cross the gangplank to board; the ladder leaves. `::pc` shows your points and wins.

## The game

- **Four portals** — purple, blue, yellow, red — start shielded. Every half-minute or so a
  shield falls (the Void Knight calls it out) and that portal can be attacked. Pests keep
  pouring from every standing portal; **spinners** heal open portals, so kill them first.
- **Defend the Void Knight** in the centre. Pests that reach him gnaw him down; if he falls,
  the game is lost. Some pests spawn inside the fort beside him.
- **Splatters** burst for 20 when they die — don't kill them in your face. **Shifters** blink
  to your side, **torchers** cast, **defilers** fling from range.
- You have **twenty minutes**. Destroy all four portals to win.
- **Activity**: hitting pests and portals earns it and it decays over time. Finish with at
  least 50 or the knights notice your lack of zeal and pay nothing.

Dying inside is safe; you're returned to the outpost. The squire in the fort lets you leave
early.

## Rewards

Spend Pest Control points with the **Void Knight** at the outpost (Exchange): void knight top /
robe / mace (250), gloves (150), the three void helms (200 each), and **elite** top / robe
(200 each plus the plain piece). No coins.
