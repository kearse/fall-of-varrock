Every vendor in the realm, in one place. For how the market *works* - instant-open shops, tab strips, the Trading Post - see [Trading Post & the market](/wiki/trading-post-and-shops). This page is the reference: **who sells what, where, and for how much**.

Every coin shop also **buys back what it stocks at 70% of value** (right-click an inventory item while the shop is open). The [Trading Post](/wiki/trading-post-and-shops) buys everyday commodities at the same 70% and the general store buys odds and ends under 500 gp; gear, crafted goods and reward-shelf items are sold to other players on the Grand Exchange. The ticket/reward vendors never buy back.

## Getting there

The market is the **Lumbridge courtyard**, just east of the castle's east wall - two facing shop rows with a walkable aisle between them. The fastest route is the **teleport portal → Basics → "Market / Shops"**, which lands you in the middle of the aisle. Home teleport puts you a few steps away.

The market belongs to Lumbridge, the Last Free City - it never closes. The war is fought *out* of Lumbridge, not against it - see [the war explained](/wiki/the-war-explained).

## The west row - combat & basics

Five vendors line the west side of the aisle, facing east.

### Melee Shop (Horvik)

Two tabs. **Weapons** stocks every combination of bronze, iron, steel, black, mithril and adamant in daggers, swords, scimitars, longswords, maces, battleaxes, warhammers and 2h swords, all at standard value - the shop **caps at adamant**; rune and dragon come from smithing, drops and [the Forge](/wiki/the-forge). The **Rank armour** tab opens the Apprentice's Armoury (below). Buys back its own stock.

### Ranged Shop (Lowe)

Leather, hardleather and studded sets, green d'hide body/chaps/vambraces, and bows up to **maple** (short and long). Arrows: bronze and iron in bulk, steel, mithril, and **adamant arrows at 120 gp each** - priced deliberately above value so Fletching stays worth training. Bronze/iron/steel darts too.

### Magic Shop (Zaff)

Elemental staves (air, water, earth, fire) and runes **capped at death**: air/water/earth/fire/mind/body in bulk, chaos and cosmic, nature and law, with **death runes at a 270 gp premium**. Teleport tablets (Varrock, Lumbridge, Falador, Camelot) at 100 gp. **Talk-to** Zaff and he'll also lift you straight to the rune altar.

### Prayer Shop (Monk)

Bones and big bones only. Higher bones are deliberately not sold - Prayer is earned, not bought. Bring your bones to the **prayer altar east of the fountain** for boosted xp.

### Crafting Shop (Gem Trader)

Silver and gold bars, uncut sapphire/emerald/ruby/diamond, all four jewellery moulds, needle, thread, chisel, leather and soft clay.

## The east row - skilling & rewards

Five more vendors face them across the aisle.

### Skilling Shop (Wydin)

**Tools** tab: pickaxes and axes bronze through **rune**, tinderbox, chisel, hammer, knife, needle, shears, glassblowing pipe. **Materials** tab: starter feedstock - copper/tin/iron ore, clay, coal, bronze and iron bars, flax, bow string, leather, thread, wool, feathers, logs up to willow, molten glass, soda ash, sand.

### Fishing Shop (Gerrant)

**Supplies** tab: nets, rods, fly rod, lobster pot, harpoon, bait and feathers in bulk. **Fish** tab: cooked food up to **swordfish** (at a 160 gp premium), plus bread, chicken, trout, salmon, tuna and lobster - everything above swordfish is sold **raw only** (monkfish, shark, sea turtle, manta ray, anglerfish, karambwan): the Cooking gate.

### Herblore Shop (Jatix)

The biggest tab strip in the market - eight stores in one window: **allotment & flower seeds** (25 kinds), **herb seeds** (guam through torstol), **tree & fruit seeds** (acorn to calquat, palm, spirit seed), **special seeds**, **clean herbs** (guam through torstol), **secondaries** (eyes of newt, unicorn horn dust, snape grass, wines of Zamorak and the rest), **unfinished potions**, and **farm tools**.

### Construction Shop (Sawmill Operator)

Planks (regular, oak, teak, mahogany), nails, bolts of cloth, saw, hammer, soft clay, limestone bricks and marble blocks.

### Rewards Shop (Valaine)

One reward tab, one ticket currency: **Vote Tickets** are a **tradeable item**, so non-voters buy them off voters (nobody sells them for coins - voting is the only faucet). The old Boss-Ticket shelf is gone: Boss Tickets were retired in September 2026 - bosses pay in drops, not tokens.

## The general store

The classic **general store by the bridge** (shop keeper and assistant behind the counter) sells pots, jugs, buckets, tinderboxes, shears, knives, chisels, hammers and spades - and it **buys odds and ends** (any tradeable worth up to 500 gp) at 70% of value, the market's junk-disposal counter. Anything dearer than that is for other players.

## Rank armour - the Apprentice's Armoury

The **Smithing Apprentice** in the Lumbridge cellar (beside the mine) - also reachable through Horvik's **Rank armour** tab - sells armour scaled to your [feudal rank](/wiki/titles-and-citizenship). Your title sets the best metal on the shelf, from iron up to **rune**, and only the lesser pieces are sold: med helms, chainbodies, platelegs and sq shields. Platebodies, full helms and kiteshields must be smithed - or won.

## The end-game vendors

The serious money stands at the **south end of the market**.

### The Warlord's Armoury (Quartermaster)

The **Quartermaster** takes [war-supply hand-ins](/wiki/supplying-the-war) on Talk-to, and his one shop tab is the **Barrows wing** (coins - the mid-game gp sink): every brother's helm, body and legs at **12,000,000 gp** apiece, every brothers' weapon at **20,000,000 gp**. Priced far above a [Barrows crypt run](/wiki/bosses-overview) on purpose - the crypt is the smart path, the shelf is bad-luck insurance for the rich.

**Boss Tickets are retired** (September 2026). The six ticket tabs that used to hang here - megarares, GWD bases, Justiciar and Inquisitor, crystal gear, charged weapons, BIS accessories and the 3rd age relics - are gone: every one of those items now comes only from the boss or war route that drops it (see [Path to end game](/wiki/path-to-end-game)), and none of them can ever be alched or sold to an NPC - they change hands between players. The Bandos and Armadyl bases for the [Royal Smith's war-forge](/wiki/war-forging) come from God Wars.

### PK Rewards (Emblem Trader)

The PvP gear chase, paid in **Blood Money** - earned only from killing **real players** (25 + 3 per combat level of the victim; [PK bots](/wiki/pk-bots-and-highwaymen) pay none). Also opens with `::pkshop`. Four gear tabs; full detail in [PK rewards & stats](/wiki/pk-rewards-and-stats). A fifth **Buy Blood Money** tab sells Blood Money for **800 gp each** (no buyback) - the coin ceiling on the PvP gear (a 3,000 BM whip caps at ~2.4M gp, a 15,000 BM godsword at ~12M).

**Supplies**: shark 5, prayer potion(4) 20, ranging potion(4) 25, super restore(4) 30, saradomin brew(4) 35, super combat(4) 40.

## Beyond the market

- **The Trading Post** (`::market`) - the commodities counter (runes, ores, bars, logs, food, herbs, gems, essence, planks); its shelf lists only what players have sold. See [Trading Post & the market](/wiki/trading-post-and-shops).
- **Donor Store** (`::donorstore`) - cosmetic rares for **Donor Points**: partyhats 900 (white 1,350), santa hat 1,350, h'ween masks 700. Tradeable, stat-less. See [Bonds & membership](/wiki/bonds-and-membership).
- **Commander's Regalia** (`::prestigeshop`) - the **sanguine Torva** cosmetic set for **Prestige** points: full helm 75, platebody and platelegs 100 each. Commanders only earn Prestige.
- **Aggie's dyes** (`::dyes`) - red, blue and green dye at 1,000,000 gp, purple at 2,500,000. Used on a Champion's Cape to recolour it (the dye and the old cape are both consumed).

## What's deliberately not sold

The gaps in the shelves are the game design: rune-plus melee gear (smith it, forge it or win it), high bones (train Prayer), cooked food above swordfish (train Cooking), potions above the reward shelves (train Herblore), and the war-forged sets - **Torva, Masori and Ancestral come only from the [Royal Smith](/wiki/war-forging)**. For the full route to every rare, see [Path to end game](/wiki/path-to-end-game).
