Combat spells across all four books work - including sticky autocast - plus a wide set of utility magic. This page covers what's live and the details that matter.

## Utility spells (Standard book)

| Spell | Effect | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High / Low Alchemy | Item → coins (60% / 40% of value) | Bonds can't be alched |
| Superheat Item | Smelt ore without a furnace | Bronze/iron/mithril/adamant/rune recipes, Smithing XP included |
| Enchant Jewellery | Gem jewellery → enchanted | One unified spell - recoil, dueling, forging, life, wealth rings + amulets |
| Bones to Bananas / Peaches | All bones in pack → food | |
| Telekinetic Grab | - | Not yet wired |

## PK spells

- **Vengeance** (Lunar, Magic 94) - reflect 75% of the next hit back at your attacker. 30-second cooldown. "Taste vengeance!"
- **Tele Block** (Magic 85) - blocks the target's teleports (spells *and* jewellery) for 5 minutes.
- **Freezes** - Bind (~5s), Snare (~10s), Entangle (~15s), plus the Ancient ice spells with their own freeze timers.
- **Curses** - Confuse/Weaken/Curse (5% stat drain) and Vulnerability/Enfeeble/Stun (10%). They currently always land.

## Teleports

City teleports work on **all four books** - Modern plus 8 Ancient, 7 Lunar and 9 Arceuus destinations, each with its book's cast animation. For everything else there's the [home teleport portal](/wiki/teleport-portal).

## Where runes come from

Basic elemental and mind runes are shop-bought in the Lumbridge market. **High-tier runes are deliberately not sold** - chaos, nature, law, death, blood, soul and wrath come from:

- **[Wizard Tower](/wiki/wizard-tower)** runs - the main rune faucet
- The **Trading Post** (`::market`) seed stock - nature, law, death, blood, cosmic, astral at cache value
- Monster drops (every NPC uses its real OSRS table)

## Elemental staves

Wielding an elemental staff is an **endless supply of its own rune** - it isn't required in your pack and it isn't consumed. Staves of air/water/earth/fire, their battlestaff and mystic versions, and the combination staves all count:

| Staff | Supplies |
| --- | --- |
| Air / Water / Earth / Fire (staff, battlestaff, mystic) | That element |
| Mist / Dust / Smoke | Air + water / air + earth / air + fire |
| Mud / Lava / Steam | Water + earth, fire + earth, water + fire |

**Autocast** needs a staff in your hands. Cast a combat spell on a target and it stays selected - you keep casting it until you switch weapons or unequip. Casting with a non-staff weapon still works, it's just a single cast.

## Spellbook access

Ancient, Lunar and Arceuus need the Wizard Tower unlock - see [Unlocking the magic books](/wiki/unlocking-spellbooks). Switch with `::spellbook`.
