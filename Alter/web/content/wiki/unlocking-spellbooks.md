The **Standard spellbook is always available**. The three special books - **Ancient** (Ice Barrage and friends), **Lunar** (Vengeance) and **Arceuus** - are sealed on every new account, and no amount of gold opens them. You unlock them by deed:

## The unlock path

1. Finish the [Recruit Trials](/wiki/recruit-trials) (your automatic starter quest).
2. The **War-Prep chain** continues from there: Vannaka drills you to **Prayer 37** at the Lumbridge church altar - he supplies 120 dragon bones for it - so you have Protect from Magic.
3. Vannaka then hands you a mage kit: mystic fire staff, mystic robes, a full rune stock (500 air / 300 water / 300 earth / 300 fire elemental runes plus 500 mind / 300 chaos / 150 death), and **100 noted prayer potions** to keep Protect from Magic up.
4. Follow the marker to the **Void Knight** at the [Wizard Tower](/wiki/wizard-tower) bridge (`::wizardtower`), enter the tower, fight up all three floors, kill **Archmage Sedridor**, and take his **grimoire**.
5. Return to Vannaka - the marker leads you back - for the debrief. He pays you **50,000 coins**, the exact price of your next rank.
6. Follow the marker to **Duke Horacio** and buy that rank - the rank-up (and the heavier armour tier it unlocks) completes the quest.

Taking the grimoire on your first clear **permanently unlocks all three special books** on your account.

## Switching books

Once unlocked, switch anytime:

- **Altar of the Occult** - in the Lumbridge market courtyard, just west of the fountain. Left-click **Venerate** for the picker menu, or right-click straight to **Ancient / Lunar / Arceuus**. A prayer altar stands on the fountain's east side, so a full book-and-prayers reset is one trip.
- `::spellbook` - opens the same picker menu from anywhere
- `::spellbook ancient` / `::spellbook lunar` / `::spellbook arceuus` / `::spellbook normal`

Switching clears your autocast, so re-select your spell after. Locked books show as "(locked)" in the menu with a reminder that the Tower seals them.

## Why it's gated

Ancient magic is the PK meta - barrage freezes, blood healing - and Vengeance swings every bridging fight. Locking them behind the Tower means every barrager you meet in the wilderness earned the book, and new players get a clear, single goal for their first hours: *clear the Tower*.
