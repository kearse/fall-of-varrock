Two free claims reset every real calendar day. Both build **streaks** - miss a day and the streak resets.

## Daily claim - `::daily`

- **Coins:** 50,000 base **+ 10,000 per consecutive day**, capped at 250,000 gp.
- **Plus** 1 Vote ticket.

At a full streak that's a quarter-million gp a day just for logging in.

## Vote claim - `::claimvote`

- **Vote tickets:** 2 base **+ 1 per consecutive day** of voting.
- `::vote` explains the vote sites (see also the [Vote page](/vote) on this site).
- In the `::vote` window, a site's button turns into a gold **Claim** once your vote is confirmed - click it to collect your tickets right there (it does the same thing as typing `::claimvote`).

The two streaks are tracked separately - you can keep a daily streak alive without voting and vice versa.

## What tickets are worth

**Vote tickets** (like Boss tickets) are **tradeable, stackable items** - currency you can hold, bank, sell to other players, or spend at the ticket reward shops in the Lumbridge market. Because they're items, they're also risked like anything else you carry - bank them.

Note: "tomorrow" means the next calendar day, not 24 hours after your last claim - claiming at 11pm and again at 12:01am is two days of streak.
