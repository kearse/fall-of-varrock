**Bonds** are the bridge between the [store](/store) and the in-game economy - bought with real money on the site, tradeable to other players for gold, and redeemable for membership or Donor Points. They're how a player who never spends real money can still earn membership with gp.

## The bond lifecycle

1. A player buys a bond on the website; it's delivered in-game as a **tradeable item**.
2. Tradeable bonds move player-to-player freely - sell one for gold, buy one with gold.
3. To use one, right-click → **Redeem**. You'll be asked to confirm, because claiming is **permanent**: the bond becomes untradeable and can never be traded again.
4. Take the untradeable bond to the **Bond Merchant** in the Lumbridge market (or use `::bonds`) and choose:

- **30 days of membership**, or
- **450 Donor Points**

## Rules that keep bonds fair

- **No shop will ever buy or sell bonds** - not the Trading Post, not the general store. They move only between players, so their gp price is set purely by the market.
- Bonds **can't be alched** - no shortcut to minting gold from them.
- Redemptions are logged.

## Membership

Membership comes in tiers - **Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond** - via the [store](/store) or bond redemption (bonds grant Bronze). Time **stacks**: redeeming while active extends your expiry. Perks include the companion auto-loot toggle, Discord roles via account linking, and the growing donor perk set listed on the store page.

The design intent, stated plainly: membership is a convenience-and-cosmetics lane, not a power lane. Gear, spellbooks and titles are earned in the war like everyone else.
