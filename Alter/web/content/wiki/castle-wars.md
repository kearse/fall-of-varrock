**Castle Wars** is the classic team war, fought on the **real Castle Wars arena**: Saradomin's
castle in the south-east, Zamorak's in the north-west, and a battlefield in between. Steal the
enemy's **standard** (their flag) off the top of their keep, carry it across the field, and touch
it to **your own standard** to score. Most captures when the horn sounds after **20 minutes** wins.

You never fight short-handed here: both armies are **filled to 10 a side with soldiers** (bots).
One real player is enough to start a war — every human who joins mid-game simply takes a
soldier's place, so it's always a full battle.

## Getting in

Use `::cw` (or walk to the Castle Wars lobby) and step through a **god portal**:

- **Saradomin portal** — fight in blue for Saradomin.
- **Zamorak portal** — fight in red for Zamorak.
- **Guthix portal** — let the balance decide; you're sent to whichever side needs you.

The portal drops you into your team's **underground waiting room**, just like the original —
a countdown to the next war shows **on screen above your chatbox** (and during the war it shows
the time remaining and the live score). The war begins about a minute after the first soldier
queues; the **exit portal** in the waiting room takes you back to the lobby if you change your
mind. If a war is already raging, a lobby portal throws you **straight into the fight** — a
filler soldier steps aside for you.

Your own gear comes with you (deaths are safe), but your cape is set aside and you wear your
team's **hooded cloak** until the war ends. It's returned when you leave, even after a crash.

## How the war works

1. **You spawn in your castle's spawn room** (keep level 1). Grab **bandages** from the tables —
   they heal and restore run energy. Pass through your team's **energy barrier** to head out.
   The barrier only lets your own team through.
2. **Cross the field — or go under it.** The enemy standard flies at the **top of their keep**.
   The direct route is through their **front gates** (open them — or fight through while your
   soldiers hold the line). The sneaky route is the **tunnels**: mid-field trapdoors drop you
   into the passages under the battlefield, surfacing **behind the enemy front line**. Each
   castle also has a side cave ladder down, and the deeper tunnels can be **mined open**
   through the rubble.
3. **Capture the standard.** Click it to seize it. The banner takes both hands (your weapon and
   shield go to your pack — keep two inventory spaces free), so your team must cover your escape.
4. **Run it home.** Touch your **own standard** with the enemy banner to **score**. Every capture
   pays the capturer a bonus on the spot.
5. **Guard your own flag.** If a flag carrier is killed, the standard falls where they died:
   the owning team clicks it to **return it home instantly**; the attackers click it to pick it
   up and keep running.

Dying is **completely safe** — you keep everything and respawn in your spawn room a moment later.
Fallen soldiers (bots) rejoin the lines after a short delay, so the armies stay at full strength
all war long.

## Rewards

Everything pays in **coins**:

| Result | Payout |
| --- | --- |
| Flag capture (to the capturer) | 25,000 |
| Win | 50,000 |
| Draw | 20,000 |
| Loss | 10,000 |

The lobby **scoreboard** tracks the current war, the last result, and your career wins and
captures.

## Tips

- **Two free inventory slots** before you go flag-running — the banner forces your weapon and
  shield into your pack.
- Bandages are war supplies: they only work **inside the arena**, so stock up from the tables in
  the castle, not for the bank.
- Your soldiers fight a **real war**: defenders hold the courtyard, return your dropped standard
  and hunt down enemy flag carriers; assault squads **breach the enemy gates**; flankers take the
  **tunnels** and surface behind the line — and each side fields a **flag runner** who will climb
  the keep, steal the standard and score it. Watch your own flag: the enemy's runner is coming.
- Head-on pushes stall against a defended gate. **Take the tunnels** and pop up behind the line —
  that's how wars get won.
- Want out mid-war? The **portal in your spawn room** returns you to the lobby (your cape comes
  back at the door).
