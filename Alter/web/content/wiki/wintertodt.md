The Wintertodt is exactly the OSRS fight. Rounds run back to back: the storm rises, the
four braziers must burn, and the beast's energy falls while they do. Between rounds the
pyromancers rest for a minute.

## How it works

1. **Enter** the Doors of Dinh (50 Firemaking). Crates by the door lend an axe, a knife, a
   hammer, a tinderbox and unfinished rejuvenation potions if you forgot yours.
2. **Chop bruma roots** (any axe you can use). Optionally **fletch** them with a knife into
   kindling for more points and XP.
3. **Light** your corner's brazier (tinderbox, 25 points), then **feed** it: roots 10 points,
   kindling 25. Firemaking XP scales with your level.
4. The Wintertodt fights back: **cold** seeps into everyone north of the doors, **snowfall**
   marks a patch and strikes it, braziers **blow out** or **shatter** (fix with a hammer, 25
   points, Construction XP) and pyromancers get **frozen**. Pick a bruma herb, mix it into an
   unfinished potion and use it on a downed pyromancer to revive them (30 points).
5. Warm clothing (pyromancer pieces, warm gloves, bruma torch, fire/infernal cape, Santa
   gear...) cuts every cold hit, and so do more lit braziers.

Dying inside is safe. Leaving through the doors resets your points.

## Rewards

When the Wintertodt is subdued, everyone inside gets **Firemaking XP (100 × level)** and a
**supply crate per 500 points** (with a proportional chance of one more). Each crate holds
three rolls of supplies — logs, ores, herbs, seeds, fish, gems, essence, bones — plus a shot
at the **burnt page** (1/45), the **pyromancer outfit** pieces, **warm gloves** and the
**bruma torch** (1/150 each), an empty **tome of fire** (1/1000) and the **phoenix** pet
(1/5000). Your subdued count and best score are kept. `::wt` shows the round's state.
