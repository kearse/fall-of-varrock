**Zulrah** is the second fight of the rebuilt roster: the great serpent of Zul-Andra, fought
**solo in your own instance**. It's a switch fight — Zulrah cycles through three forms, and
each form is only vulnerable to the right style. Bring **ranged and magic** (melee never
reaches it), venom protection if you have it, and food you trust.

## Getting there

Teleport portal → **Bosses → Zulrah** lands you at the Zul-Andra dock. Type **`::zulrah`**
and the priestess rows you to the shrine. Dying inside is a **safe death** — you wake up
at home with your gear.

## The three forms

| Form | Colour | Attacks | Attack it with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Serpentine | Green | Rapid ranged | **Magic** (it's weak to it) — never melee |
| Magma | Red | Melee lunge | **Melee or magic** — ranged bounces off |
| Tanzanite | Blue | Ranged AND magic | **Ranged** — magic bounces off |

Zulrah follows one of **four fixed rotations** of form + position (centre, south, east,
west). Learn the rotations and you always know what's next — the first phase is always
the green form at centre.

- **Green / blue phases**: pray against the incoming style (blue alternates both — watch
  the projectiles) and keep dealing damage.
- **Red phase**: it only melees. Stand a couple of tiles off; when it rears toward your
  tile you have a beat to step away — the lunge hits 20-41 **through prayer** and stuns
  you if you're still adjacent.
- **The jad phase** (once per rotation, late): Zulrah alternates ranged and magic **every
  attack** — switch your overhead each hit, exactly like Jad.

## Clouds and snakelings

- **Toxic clouds**: Zulrah spits fume clouds onto fixed spots around the shrine. Standing
  in one chips you constantly — the rotation always leaves safe ground; stand there.
- **Snakelings**: venomous snakes burrow up next to you (up to three at once). They die to
  a single hit — swat them fast, their bites can envenom you.
- Most of Zulrah's attacks — and every snakeling bite — can **envenom** you. Anti-venom
  is worth its inventory slot.

## Loot

The drop table rolls **twice per kill**, always including a pile of **Zulrah's scales**.
The chase drops, all Collection Log entries:

| Drop | Rarity (per kill) |
|---|---|
| Tanzanite fang / Magic fang / Serpentine visage | 1/125 each roll |
| Uncut onyx | 1/200 |
| Jar of swamp | 1/500 |
| Tanzanite / Magma mutagen | 1/1500 |
| Pet snakeling | 1/1000 |

Kills count toward `::kc`. After the kill, a portal rises beside the shrine to take
you back to Zul-Andra.
